An entry for the make.fun bounty **One-Sentence Absurd Invention
Pitch**
(https://make.fun/bounty/u-suroso-69-one-sentence-absurd-invention-pitch-5326c34a).

`main.py` (Python standard library only) writes one sentence — at
most 220 characters — to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Introducing the Doorway Reminder — a door frame that notes why
> you stood up and whispers it back the moment you arrive in the
> kitchen with no idea what you came for.

For everyone who has ever crossed a room with total conviction and
arrived with none.
